---
name: Manual league results and private profile identity
description: Why free-text manual results cannot authorize or fully attribute private player profiles.
---

Private league profiles require a player row from a tournament linked to that league, even when standings also include names entered only through manual results. Do not turn a manual-only name into a private profile route without a stable league-scoped identity and an explicit link to its events.

**Why:** A manual result's event label can refer to an external event or match multiple league tournaments. Treating the label alone as a roster identity or event association risks showing a misleading history or authorizing the wrong profile.

**How to apply:** For manual-only profiles or historical attendance, introduce explicit league/player and event associations with owner authorization. Keep ambiguous manual points separate instead of guessing which tournament earned them.
